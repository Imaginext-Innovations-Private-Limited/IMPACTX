Responsible AI & Citizen Privacy Protection

Welfare Scheme Navigator is engineered for community trust. We adhere to strict architectural safeguards:

  • No Sensitive PII: We never ask for or store 12-digit Aadhaar numbers, PAN cards, or bank credentials.
  • Deterministic Eligibility: Eligibility rules are executed by pure Python logic, not generative AI approximations.
  • Source Grounding: RAG retrieval references verified PDFs and government gazettes with page citations.
  • Indicative Assessment Only: Final approval is always determined by the competent government authority.
Mandatory Disclaimer
Welfare Scheme Navigator provides indicative eligibility assessments only based on published guidelines. It does not constitute guaranteed government approval. Final approval is subject to document verification by competent government authorities.
