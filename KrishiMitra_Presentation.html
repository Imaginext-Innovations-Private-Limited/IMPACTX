The Problem

The Daily Struggles of Smallholder Farmers

Agronomic & Pesticide Loss

20% to 35% of crops are destroyed by misidentified pests. Farmers take unreadable bottles to retail shops, receive wrong pesticides, and overdose their crops with no knowledge of barrel dilution or safe waiting periods.

Market & Price Asymmetry

Middlemen exploit farmers at the farm gate with low prices. Farmers lack real-time visibility into neighboring APMC prices and historical seasonal patterns to know when and where to sell.

Digital & Language Barriers

Traditional agritech apps are complex, text-heavy, and built in English. Over 80% of rural farmers prefer spoken vernacular dialogue and familiar tools like WhatsApp over downloading complex new apps.

🗣️ Speaker Script: "A farmer in rural Karnataka doesn't have an agronomy degree. When pests attack, they visit a local shop, buy whatever the dealer pushes, spray it right before a heavy downpour, and watch their entire investment wash away. KrishiMitra replaces this guesswork with instant intelligence."

Core Feature 6

Whole-Year Farm Balance Sheet & Wealth Planner

Farm Ledger Balance Sheet

Tracks every expense categorized into Cultivation, Irrigation, Harvesting, and Household, alongside crop revenues to compute the true Net Profit per acre.

Eliminates hidden losses in labor and diesel maintenance.

4-Bucket Wealth Allocation

Helps farmers apportion lump-sum harvest earnings into 4 clear reserves: Household Living, Next Cultivation Reserve, Harvest Fund, and Life Commitments.

Prevents debt traps by reserving capital before spending.

KCC & Loan Tracker

Monitors active Kisan Credit Card (KCC) loans and alerts farmers before due dates to lock in the Government's 3% prompt repayment interest subvention.

Saves up to ₹4,000 in interest penalties each year.
🗣️ Speaker Script: "Most farmers experience a cash surge during harvest, spend it on social commitments, and then borrow at 24% interest for the next planting season. Our 4-bucket wealth planner keeps them financially resilient."

Resilience
